# ENEMY_DESIGN.md: the TTK pivot, the roster ledger, the economy rebalance

Dated 2026-07-29.

The commission, in the creator's words: we drifted into survivors-style low-hp fodder while the game became a roguelite. In the reference genre you can get overpowered, but at run start you one-shot almost nothing. This document is the pivot, run to the number: a binding TTK doctrine, a grading of every body in the game against it, and the economic consequences.

Two evidence rules, binding on every line below:

1. Findings are claims. Every claim about MBM code carries file:line. Every computed number shows its arithmetic. Every library claim carries the report path (research/game-systems/<report>/report.md) and section.
2. Outside-game claims are SOURCED or RECALLED, never recalled-as-measured. Where the creator's memory of another game does the work, it is labeled recall and graded against what the library actually records.

## 1. WHERE THE GAME ACTUALLY SITS TODAY: the fodder measurement

At act 1 room 0 (threat m=0), per the roster weights at js/data.js:291 (total 110) and base per-hit damage of 13.2 / 4.0 per gob / 24.0 (js/data.js:43,54,65 with the damage stat at js/data.js:18, js/stats.js:194, applied at js/game.js:810):

- Max one-shots 54.5% of the roster by weight: squirrel 22 + rabbit 20 + bat 10 + ticktot 8 = 60/110.
- Gus one-chops 65.5%: the same 60 plus chipchoir 12 = 72/110.
- Junie one-gobs 7.3%: ticktot only, 8/110. Even a full two-gob volley (8 damage) kills nothing else.

So the survivors-fodder feel is real but it is narrower than it looks: it belongs to two of three scouts and only to the opening rooms. By mid act 1 (room 4 of 8, m=2.1, hpScale = 1 + 1.16(2.1) + 0.008(2.1)^2 = 3.471 per js/data.js:327, threat per js/data.js:344-345) the cheapest body in the game, ticktot, is 4 x 3.471 = 13.88 hp, which exceeds Max's 13.2 stone. One-hit fraction: Max 0%, Junie 0%, Gus 7.3% (ticktots only). The game already IS a few-hit game from minute three onward. The two actual defects are:

1. The opening is a mow-down for Max and Gus (majority of the roster in one hit), which sells the wrong genre in the first ninety seconds.
2. The transition out of it is an accident of the quadratic hp curve, not an authored graduation, and the same curve keeps going: at act 2 entry (m=4.2, hpScale 6.013) the audited mid-run loadout (weapon level 3, Trail Mix 2, Max 21.82 per stone) needs 37 stones for a beaver (793.7 hp: ceil(793.7 / 21.82) = 37 stones, (37 - 1) x 0.42 = 15.1 s of chained fire on one body under the pinned single-projectile fire model, section 3) and 22 for a stiltheron. The base trash hp spread is 132/4 = 33x (js/data.js:153-195), and a uniform multiplier preserves that 33x forever, so the top of the roster drifts from "a few hits" into sponge territory while the bottom drifts out of one-shot range. Neither end is where anyone put it on purpose.

Weighted mean opening TTK, the number the pivot moves: Max = (22x1 + 20x1 + 26x3 + 10x1 + 8x1 + 12x2 + 12x2)/110 = 186/110 = 1.69 stones per kill. Gus = 148/110 = 1.35 chops. Junie = (22x3 + 20x4 + 26x10 + 10x3 + 8x1 + 12x4 + 12x7)/110 = 576/110 = 5.24 gobs under the pinned model (2.85 volleys, 314/110, if both gobs of every volley land: the compressed read).

## 2. WHAT FODDER BOUGHT US, honestly

The survivors model is not stupid; density serves three functions the pivot must re-house, not lose. The framing that kill cadence is the income and decision cadence is MBM's OWN SYNTHESIS, not a library quote: it is built from the survivors floor's recorded rows (XP/level progression with a level-up event, research/game-systems/survivors-horde-systems-floor/report.md S3; front-loaded level-up cadence and the sparse-opening-to-peak-finale escalation shape, S5) plus the Brotato ledger's recorded fact that slain enemies drop materials (research/game-systems/brotato-experience-ledger/report.md S4). No floor sentence says it; the synthesis is ours and is marked as such.

1. The badge shower. Act 1 income measured 100.7 badges/min decomposes as roughly 51.5 chaff kills/min x 1.45 badges/kill plus lumps: one 100 badge boss lump and an estimated 3 elites x 10 x 1.45 = 43.5 (elites pay 10x xp, js/game.js:979). Arithmetic: (552 - 100 - 43.5)/1.45 = 408.5/1.45 = 281.7, about 282 kill-equivalents over 5.48 min = 51.5/min (552 raw over 5.48 min per BUILD_ECONOMY_3.md:471-475; xp credited at js/game.js:717-718). Cut kills per minute and you cut income per minute in the same ratio unless weight moves elsewhere.
2. The power readout. Both genre class floors ban floating damage numbers outright (research/game-systems/roguelite-dungeon-systems-floor/report.md S9; research/game-systems/top-down-action-adventure-systems-floor/report.md S4). MBM as shipped VIOLATES both cited floors: per-hit floating damage numbers render by default (js/game.js:843-844, gated on SETTINGS.dmgNums, default true at js/ui.js:11, options toggle at js/ui.js:1356 and 2108), plus burn and rend tick numbers (js/director.js:1914-1915, 1929-1930). The readout argument is therefore conditional: once the pivot adopts the floor ban (remove SETTINGS.dmgNums or default it off, CREATOR FLAG 6), bodies dying faster becomes the ONLY way the player reads "my build got stronger"; until then it is the primary readout, not the only one. Either way fodder is the ruler, and a few-hit roster keeps the ruler only if hits-to-kill visibly shrinks across the night.
3. Screen texture and the kill sensation. COOP_DESIGN.md:455-459 states the product truth: the sensation sold is volume of kills. Room budgets already encode it: 55 to 70 authored spawns per combat room (js/rooms.js:181-183), 90 second room ceiling (.claude/agents/release-gate.md:46-50).

What the library records about rewards, stated at its actual strength: room-scoped placed rewards ARE the class's reward cadence. Hades pays roughly one reward per chamber read off the door symbol, Isaac routes items through Treasure/boss/shop placements, premium meta currency is boss-primary (research/game-systems/roguelite-dungeon-systems-floor/report.md S5; research/game-systems/hades-experience-ledger/report.md S4, S9). No report discusses kill density dropping or rewards replacing it; the inference that room-scoped rewards are the correct RE-HOUSING vehicle when kills/min falls is this doctrine's own, marked as such. MBM already owns the vehicles: room-clear coin pay (js/room.js:770, armed combat rooms only per js/room.js:749-770), elite chest plus 10x gems (js/game.js:979-980), boss lumps (js/game.js:981-985), and two dead constants authored for exactly this and never wired: DATA.COINS.elite (12) and DATA.COINS.boss (60) at js/data.js:554-555 have no reader anywhere in js/. The pivot's economy rule: as kills/min falls, income weight shifts from per-corpse gems toward clear/elite/boss lumps, and the dead constants get wired as part of that shift. Every income number downstream of kill rate (the full list at BUILD_ECONOMY_3.md:38-41, 471-490, 494-524, 528-545, 822-839) is re-measured after the pivot, none is carried forward.

## 3. THE TARGET TTK DOCTRINE

TTK is authored in HITS AT ACT ENTRY with the act-reference loadout, and hp is DERIVED from the band times expected player damage. Never the reverse again. Reference loadouts: act 1 entry = base weapons (Max 13.2/stone); act 2 entry = weapon level 3 + Trail Mix 2 (Max 21.82/stone, the audited assumption); act 3 entry loadout is unaudited and must be measured before the roster ledger fills its column (CREATOR FLAG).

THE PINNED FIRE MODEL, binding on all three chapters: one projectile per trigger per scout. One Max stone per 0.42 s, one Junie gob per 0.16 s, one Gus chop per 0.55 s; seconds chained on one body = (hits - 1) x cadence. This is deliberately conservative. The act 2 reference slingshot at level 3 actually fires 2 stones per volley (the level 2 upgrade is +1 stone, js/data.js:45-46) and Junie's volley fires 2 gobs, so on a focused body real kill time compresses up to 2x for both of them. That compression is real, it is treated IDENTICALLY for Max and Junie (neither gets to count their second projectile), and it is MEASURED by the fleet run (measurement plan, run class 1), never modeled. Anywhere a verdict would flip under full compression, the flip is stated in place (the elite retune in the roster ledger is the one known case).

Bands are stated in Max stones as the reference unit; a body is in band for Junie and Gus when its kill time under their starting weapons falls within the same seconds envelope under the pinned model. HITS GOVERN; the seconds column is advisory, computed as (hits - 1) x 0.42 s at Max cadence.

Library grounding, stated honestly: no report anywhere in the library records an enemy hp table (single non-promoted lead excepted, THE LIBRARY section above), a TTK figure, or a per-role kill-time target; the survivors floor's kill-time check R3b abstains because no canon value exists (research/game-systems/survivors-horde-systems-floor/report.md S5A). What the library DOES pin: early roguelite rooms are "brief skirmishes against one or two enemies" with damage output and doors setting the whole tempo (research/game-systems/binding-of-isaac-experience-ledger/report.md S4), difficulty is depth-gated never clock-gated (research/game-systems/roguelite-dungeon-systems-floor/report.md S5), and the first weak body in SPD "dies in a hit or two" (research/game-systems/shattered-pixel-dungeon-experience-ledger/report.md S2). Therefore every numeric band below is a PROPOSAL, creator sign-off required; the shape (few-hit floor, one sanctioned one-shot class, schedule-anchored top tier) is library-grounded.

THE SANCTIONED EXCEPTION, the fly and spider class: TICKTOT (4 hp, 138 spd, js/data.js:179) is our fly. It dies to one hit from every scout including one Junie gob, in every room of every act, forever. That requires exempting ticktot from the full hp curve (authored per-act hp, not base x hpScale), which is a code change the roster ledger inherits. CHIPCHOIR (16 hp leashed packs, js/data.js:183, js/director.js:299-315) is our tiny-spider swarm: one hit for Gus, one to two for Max, four gobs for Junie (two volleys; a single volley finishes it only with burn credit, arithmetic in the ledger's chipchoir row), in act 1 only; from act 2 it graduates to SWARM-band 2 hits. Squirrel and bat are opening-only one-shots: one Max stone in act 1 rooms 0-2, authored to graduate to 2 hits by mid act 1 (making today's accidental collapse an authored beat). Nothing else in the game ever dies to one hit at act entry. PROPOSAL, all of it.

Per-role bands, hits at act entry with the act-reference loadout (PROPOSAL). Hits govern; seconds are the advisory chained-fire envelope at Max's 0.42 s cadence, (hits - 1) x 0.42:

|---|---|---|
| SWARM (ticktot, chipchoir) | 1 (the exception class) | under 0.5 s |
| RUSHER (squirrel, rabbit, bat, cindercrow, fox) | 2 to 3 | 0.42 to 0.84 s |
| BULK (doe, raccoon, pineconepuffer) | 3 to 5 | 0.84 to 1.68 s |

Consequences already visible in the numbers: cindercrow (currently 3 stones plus 4 more for the split children, 7 total) is over RUSHER band and its split children need to land at 1 stone each; beaver at 10 base stones and 37 mid-run stones is far over FORTRESS band. Under the act 2 anchors the roster ledger derives, the HIT spread compresses to 7x (beaver 7 hits / ticktot 1; band ceiling would allow 9x) and the effective HP spread to about 21x (150/7 = 21.4), against today's 33x base spread (132/4); FORTRESS identity is carried by mechanics (guard cycle, tail slap shove at js/director.js:1971-1988) rather than raw hp. Doe at 3 stones is already in BULK band; most of act 1's middle is closer to correct than the commission feared.

Two binding aggregate checks the roster ledger must run per room:
- Room throughput: weighted mean hits x authored spawns x cooldown must fit the 90 s ceiling with travel slack. Sanity arithmetic at the proposed opening mean of 2.5 stones: 60 bodies x 2.5 x 0.42 s = 63 s of pure single-target fire for Max, tight but legal before pierce (slingshot level 4, js/data.js:47) and Gus's arc multi-hit (js/game.js:1555-1571) relax it. Any room that fails this check rejects the band, not the ceiling.
- Weighted act-entry roster mean: 2.2 to 2.8 Max stones, every act (versus today's 1.69 opening). PROPOSAL. Holding the MEAN flat across acts while the CAST shifts heavier is exactly LEVEL_DESIGN's escalation law (headcount flat or falling, escalation via cast weight, LEVEL_DESIGN.md:1003-1021) restated in TTK units.

Untouched by this doctrine: the CHIP/REAL/HEAVY attack taxonomy (MOTION_DESIGN.md:324-346) governs time-to-REACT and stands as written; this chapter governs time-to-KILL only. Enemy damage values also stay put except where the roster ledger renegotiates them, because flat armour chaff-hit immunity is Gus's identity (live code: Stats.damageTaken at js/game.js:1016-1030 with armourFlat at js/stats.js:205; BUILD_ARCHITECTURE.md:434-444 recorded the same semantics at the older js/game.js:472, now stale) and dodge derivations hang on current speeds (GAP_MIN 158 on fox 140, LANE_MIN 130 on boar 340, LEVEL_DESIGN.md:771-798).

## 4. THE SCALING SHAPE: shredding is the earned late-run state

Library frame: in Isaac, shredding late is an in-run earned state, never the start state (Quality tags weight stronger items deeper, research/game-systems/binding-of-isaac-experience-ledger/report.md S9; enemy rosters and item pools step by chapter depth, same ledger S5; every run restarts at the fixed baseline), and Hades' documented intent is damage and survivability growing "roughly in step" with threat (research/game-systems/hades-experience-ledger/report.md S5). Today's MBM curve does the opposite: hpScale is quadratic (1 + 1.16m + 0.008m^2, js/data.js:327) while a normal run's damage levers are weapon levels plus three Trail Mix ranks (js/stats.js:60, 194), so relative player power FALLS all night. The audited mid-run table proves it: at act 2 entry the roster runs 2 to 37 stones. Nobody authored 37.

The shape this doctrine binds:

- ACT ENTRY (each act): the roster sits in its role bands under the act-reference loadout. This is the few-hit baseline, re-anchored per act. An on-curve player experiences act 2 and act 3 entry at the SAME hits-to-kill as act 1 entry; depth escalates through cast weight, count, and mechanics, matching the library's depth-gates-difficulty floor.
- MID-RUN: within an act, an on-curve build should be drifting one band DOWN by the act's back half (act-entry RUSHERs dying in 1 to 2, BULK in 2 to 4). Mid act 2 concretely, mid-run loadout: SWARM 1, RUSHER 2 to 3, BULK 3 to 4, FORTRESS at or under 9. PROPOSAL.
- LATE, INVESTED: shredding means act 3 chaff returning to act-1-opening kill feel (RUSHERs at 1 hit, BULK at 2) for a build that spent well on the 19-stat block's damage lattice. Once the damage-number ban lands (CREATOR FLAG 6), this falling-TTK curve IS the power readout; if hits-to-kill does not visibly shrink, the build reads dead.
- THE BACKSTOP: an uncapped weapon ladder meeting three fixed acts requires the director scaling term keyed to build depth that BUILD_ARCHITECTURE.md:1109-1110 already mandates (wave 4 item V1). This doctrine gives that term its target: it exists to keep an OVER-curve build from one-shotting outside the sanctioned exception class before act 3's back quarter, and an UNDER-curve build inside 1.5x the band ceiling. It tunes toward the bands; it never replaces them.
- INVARIANTS: enemy hp and damage never scale with player count (COOP_DESIGN.md:446-465); elites keep their own linear curve (hp = base x (1 + 0.55m) x 9, js/director.js:186-198, kept for the documented reason that the quadratic times 9 out-tanks the final boss; the CONSTANT is under measurement, roster ledger elite section); splits and summons must route through the game's single spawn path and count against room budgets. On that last one, honesty about the cite: C14 (COOP_DESIGN.md:1110-1114) claims the routing lives at js/room.js:62, but that line is the tighter-woods body multiplier (this.bodyMul), and the live split path is js/director.js:913-925 calling Game.spawnEnemy directly (js/director.js:916; spawn at js/director.js:174). C14's stated mechanism is NOT implemented at its cited line as of this code; the invariant binds the intent via the live path, and C14's cite needs updating or implementing. No META reads at fire time (I10).

## 5. THE ROLE TAXONOMY

The library contains no formal enemy role taxonomy: no report uses fodder, chaser, shooter, tank, disruptor, or spawner as role categories, and the whole recorded vocabulary is a tier ladder (basic, elite/miniboss, boss) plus VS's unbeatable-chaser finale role (absence verified across the nine reports named in THE LIBRARY section; tiers per research/game-systems/hades-experience-ledger/report.md S5, research/game-systems/brotato-experience-ledger/report.md S5). So MBM supplies its own, built from the fragments the repo already half-states (the gait table at LEVEL_DESIGN.md:771-798, the archetype cast notes, the borrowed LLR costs weak 1 / medium 2 / strong 4 / elite 8 at LEVEL_DESIGN.md:1003-1010).

Adopted structure: the library's TIER ladder (trash, elite, boss, schedule-anchored per the cross-library finding that the top threat tier is never random), crossed with seven in-house TRASH ROLES that carry the TTK bands, plus cross-cutting TAGS for mechanics that do not change targeting priority.

- SWARM (LLR 1): exists to die and calibrate the power readout. Ticktot, chipchoir.
- RUSHER (LLR 1): fast closers that price the dodge. Squirrel, rabbit, bat, cindercrow, fox (fox is the GAP_MIN knife-edge body and its 140 speed is untouchable without re-deriving LEVEL_DESIGN 4.3).
- BULK (LLR 2): walkers that occupy space and meter the player's position. Doe, raccoon, pineconepuffer.
- ARTILLERY (LLR 2, stiltheron 4): ranged pressure, the reason Lanes rooms carry risk. Owl, bogfrog, stiltheron.
- CHARGER (LLR 4): lane threats; the Funnel archetype requires one. Boar, ashelk (ashelk's body IS the derived spacing unit Er 17 / Ew 52, LEVEL_DESIGN.md:63-99, so its dimensions are frozen).
- FORTRESS (LLR 4): damage-gated timing bodies. Quarrybadger, beaver.
- DISRUPTOR (LLR 2): bodies that attack the player's plan rather than their hp. Sapperrat; THE TALLYMAN is its elite-tier expression (attacks the coin economy, js/director.js:283-294).
- TAGS (mechanics, orthogonal to role): on-death (pineconepuffer burst, cindercrow split), phase (sapperrat burrow, quarrybadger guard), pack (chipchoir leash), economy (Tallyman). Tags bind to the split/summon spawn-path invariant (section 4) and to MOTION_DESIGN's authored attack rows; a tag never moves a body's TTK band.

Every current and future roster body gets exactly one role, one LLR cost, any tags, and a per-act band cell. The roster ledger chapter grades all 18 species plus elites against this table; the encounter chapter buys waves in LLR points against it. A body that fits no role is a change request against this chapter, not a quiet eighth role.
