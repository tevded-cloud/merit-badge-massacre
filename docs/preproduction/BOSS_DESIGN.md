# BOSS_DESIGN.md: MERIT BADGE MASSACRE

This document is the boss design record for Merit Badge Massacre, assembled 2026-07-29. It exists because the five bosses shipped with mechanics that work but carry no stated reasons: numbers with no argument, phases with no doctrine, presentation spent with no note on why. Each chapter below takes one boss and answers, mechanic by mechanic, what it is for, whether the reason holds, and what 3.0 should do about it. Two evidence rules govern every claim. A claim about this codebase carries a file:line a chapter author actually read (claims marked "carried" come from the as-built inventory pass and were not re-read in the authoring session; anything neither read nor sourced is labelled UNVERIFIED). A claim from the research library carries the report path and the section it came from. Guesses are labelled guesses, and costs in days are estimates throughout. Every chapter passed an adversarial skeptic pass; the corrections are folded into the text below, not appended as errata.

# BRAMBLEBUCK (act 1, the first exam)

Grounding note. Every js/ citation in this chapter was read directly in this session unless marked "carried", which means it comes from the as-built inventory pass and was not re-read here. Library citations are research report paths under D:\Programs\GameTev\research. Day costs in section 4 are guesses and are labelled as guesses.

## 1. WHAT THIS BOSS IS FOR

Function in the night. BRAMBLEBUCK is the act 1 gate, and in room runs he is the only tier 1 boss (DATA.bossTiers, js/data.js:520-546, carried), so every night's boss ladder opens with him. The library is unambiguous that a boss gating advancement is a hard floor for this class (research/game-systems/roguelite-dungeon-systems-floor/report.md S3) and that in a resetting run structure the act 1 boss teaches the exam format while later bosses escalate pressure (research/game-design/journey-act-chapter-structure-recipes/report.md S4.2 and S5.2). That is exactly the job his three phases already do, whether or not anyone wrote it down: phase 1 teaches the ground-zone language (every boss attack in the game is a zone, js/director.js:638-641), phase 2 teaches held health with a lever you break (holdHp, js/director.js:674-699, the same machinery behind MAWMOLE's tunnels, THE BEHIND's cover, and the phase roars), and phase 3 teaches the authored punish window (a 1.5s self-stun the code comment calls "the damage window the fight is built on", js/director.js:1367, 1372-1376). Every later boss is a harder conjugation of one of those three sentences. He is also historically the wall: the tuning essay records most first-night losses happened in his den at three minutes (js/data.js:207-208). A hard end is what makes escalation payable (research/game-systems/single-room-tension-systems-floor/report.md S1A lesson 5); BRAMBLEBUCK is the first hard end the night has, and the first proof the run's build is being examined at all (research/game-systems/hades-experience-ledger/report.md S5 and S14, the build must keep pace with each biome's named boss).

Narrative role. Per STORY_DESIGN.md section 5, BRAMBLEBUCK owns beat 18, the arc beat that ends on "It was, and I want to stress this, a rabbit", and he is one of only three of twenty-one creatures with a readable name (STORY_DESIGN.md section 2.1, carried). The spine constrains him the same as everyone: he can bleed only because belief starved, and killing him is the win and the tragedy at once, unresolved. The fight as built serves the tone order (cute, then wrong, wrong stays): he is a rabbit that hops, squashes, and lifts thirty pixels like a rabbit (js/render.js:790-799), and he is also 96 units wide with a name card, a spoken line, and a warren that vents bodies. One narrative seam the code opens: the music source calls him "the jackalope boss" (js/music/src/boss_bramblebuck.js:1-7) while the doctrine set says hare and AUTHENTICITY.md's geography lock builds the roster on upper Great Lakes species, snowshoe hare included. A jackalope is a hoax that got believed, which is almost too on-the-nose for the spine (that reading is mine, and the jackalope-as-taxidermy-hoax fact is RECALLED, not sourced), but antlers on the act 1 boss would also step on OLD NOBODY's silhouette. This goes to section 5 as an open call rather than a recommendation.

## 2. THE MECHANIC LEDGER

Each entry: the mechanic with its citation, the goal it serves in one sentence, the library grounding for why that goal matters, and a verdict. Honesty note that covers the whole table: the library holds no phase doctrine (its only phase fact is that Hades marks transitions with stingers, research/game-systems/hades-experience-ledger/report.md S10), and the game-systems ledgers hold no telegraph numbers: their timing gaps are explicit (i-frame windows, animation and sample timings, loop lengths, all marked not-documented, hades ledger S11, hyper-light-drifter ledger S11, hollow-knight ledger S12), and no ledger documents an attack windup or telegraph duration anywhere, so telegraph numbers are an absence in the ledgers, not an explicit gap row. Per-tier telegraph and recovery tables do exist in research/asset-forge/boss-encounter-recipes/report.md S4.5 (found during THE BEHIND's verification); this chapter's timing judgments were made against house doctrine and were not re-checked against those tables. Where a goal below is phase or telegraph reasoning, it is house reasoning measured against house doctrine, and it says so.

1. Hop-only locomotion. No walk speed (spd falls back to 0, js/director.js:324; the data row carries only hopSpd 360, js/data.js:256); movement is a 0.42s burst then a 0.25 to 0.5s idle (hopStep, js/director.js:1197-1219). Goal: a boss whose position is continuous but whose threat is discrete, so the player can read state (where he is, which hop this is) without ever being told timing exactly. Grounding: publish the threat's state, withhold its timing; fully hidden reads unfair, fully scheduled reads as a chore (research/game-systems/single-room-tension-systems-floor/report.md S1A lesson 3). The randomized idle is the withheld timing; the hop count is the published state, and Brock's death tip "count the hops" (js/dialogue.js:116, carried) tells the player the state is worth reading. Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON. This is the best-reasoned mechanic on the boss.

2. Entrance beat: toast, roar, shake 8, hitstop 0.4, bossFlash 0.9, 4.4s name card with epithet and spoken line, scout named answer held until the line's measured length plus a gap (js/director.js:390-456, 643-649). Goal: spend boss-tier presentation on the beat that structurally earns it, the act-closing milestone. Grounding: the boss MUSIC role is reserved for the boundary beat, never an ordinary milestone (research/game-design/journey-act-chapter-structure-recipes/report.md S6.2, a music-role rule applied to the whole entrance package by analogy, my mapping, flagged the same way entry 11 flags its S7.2 analogy); boss bar and name grammar (research/game-systems/hollow-knight-experience-ledger/report.md S3). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON. One reconcile flag, not a verdict change: entrance hitstop is 0.4 while MOTION_DESIGN's screen-effect law sets boss kill hitstop at 0.300 with a rolling debt clamp of about 0.25s; whether entrance hitstop is bound by the same clamp needs a doctrine ruling (guess: it is, and 0.4 will get trimmed).

3. Phase cuts [0.6, 0.25] with a 1.2s untouchable phase-guard roar between phases (js/director.js:343, 650, 992-1047). Goal: escalation stepped by fight state, not by a clock, with a legible untouchable beat marking each step. Grounding: plateau-and-escalate stepped primarily by gameplay-event triggers such as HP thresholds and phase changes, with a clock permitted only as a sparingly used safety valve (research/audio/music-tension-pacing/report.md S8.3); [BER] specifies a 1.2 to 1.5s invulnerable transition beat (LEVEL_DESIGN.md 3.8, citing research/asset-forge/boss-encounter-recipes/report.md, carried). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON. Two flags. First, the guard's white strobe renders at 8Hz (Math.floor(t*8)%2, js/render.js:823-828), which MOTION_DESIGN's photosensitivity law names specifically and orders replaced; that fix is shared machinery, not his, but his fight is where a new player meets it first. Second, his cuts (0.60/0.25) disagree with the music triggers (see entry 12).

4. Phase 1 hopscotch stamp: every third landing spawns a circle zone r90, warn 0.35s, dmg 0.8x, knock 60, with a swing sfx and shake 2 (js/director.js:1235-1244). Goal: make the count matter (the third hop is the dangerous one) and teach the red-circle language on the cheapest possible attack before the game ever asks the player to read one that kills. Grounding: this is the exam-format-teaching job the act 1 boss owes the run (research/game-design/journey-act-chapter-structure-recipes/report.md S5.2); the ledgers hold no telegraph numbers, so the timing judgment is against house doctrine. Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON, with a tuning flag that matters at this act position: 0.35s is the shortest warn on any boss zone in the game, on the boss whose job is teaching zone reading, and it sits below MOTION_DESIGN's 0.60s threshold under which the world channel (the ground circle) is not even supposed to exist. The body channel does carry it (the landing is visible through the whole 0.42s hop, and the count gives roughly two full hops of anticipation to a player who is counting), so the attack is not unfair; it is grammatically illegal under the house's own telegraph rules. Section 4 proposes the number; section 5 asks the grammar question.

5. Phase 2 warren mounds: up to 4 stump props at the arena markers, hp pinned to player output at max(18, round(45*dmgMul)) so each reads as about three hits regardless of build (js/director.js:1248-1266). Goal: convert the mid-fight from a damage check into a target-switching check that every build experiences identically. Grounding: the boss as exam of the drafted build, power growing in step with threat (research/game-systems/hades-experience-ledger/report.md S5); the pinning is what keeps the exam about decisions rather than DPS, which is house reasoning on top of that. Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON. The hp-pinning trick is quietly one of the smartest numbers in the boss roster and should be named as a house pattern for any future objective prop.

6. Phase 2 held health while any mound lives (holdHp 'warren', js/director.js:1274; blocked hits give a dry click and gray sparks, js/game.js:801-906, carried). Goal: teach the game's core boss grammar, "the bar is parked and the lever is elsewhere", in its gentlest form (the lever is four three-hit props in plain sight). Grounding: no library phase doctrine exists (gap, flagged above); the grounding is internal: MAWMOLE's tunnels, THE BEHIND's cover, and OLD NOBODY's signs all assume a player who already understands an unmoving health bar is an instruction, and BRAMBLEBUCK is the only place that lesson can be taught cheaply. The blockHit feedback exists precisely because silent held health used to eat 13 to 19 percent of player damage unnoticed (postmortem comment, js/game.js:776-800, carried). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON, and the reason is load-bearing for two other bosses.

7. Phase 2 add venting: each living mound vents one squirrel every 3s, cap 24 living non-boss, spawns at marker-anchored mounds (js/director.js:1290-1303). Goal: pressure that scales with how slowly the player does the objective, so dawdling is punished by crowd rather than by timer. Grounding: no ledger in the read set covers adds mechanics, and no report named "boss craft harvest" exists in the library (verified during assembly); boss-encounter-recipes carries a spawn-count vocabulary (research/asset-forge/boss-encounter-recipes/report.md S3.3) but no rationale, so this is house reasoning; the marker-only spawn rule is Den doctrine compliance (LEVEL_DESIGN.md 3.8, [BER] 3.3: minions spawn only at pre-computed positions, carried). Squirrels are act 1's first trash, so the pressure is familiar bodies, not a new mechanic late, which matches the no-new-mechanics-in-the-exam rule (research/game-design/journey-act-chapter-structure-recipes/report.md S5.2). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON.

8. Phase 2 corner parking: while the warren stands he moves to the nearest marker corner and hops in place, with no attacks of any kind; hareTendWarren passes a null landing callback, so even the hopscotch stamp is off (js/director.js:1278-1288). Goal as built: staging (he clears the center for the add fight and stands next to a mound as a signpost). Grounding: none found; and the codebase itself has already convicted this pattern once, in MAWMOLE's tuning essay: a phase with nothing venting and nothing to shoot "was worse than slow, it was dead air" (js/data.js:220-223). Phase 2 avoids the dead-air charge only because squirrels vent; the boss's own body is inert scenery for a whole phase of his own fight. Verdict: HAS NO REASON YET. The reason it could carry: make his corner the hot corner. Either his in-place landings stamp small local zones (so the mound he guards must be cleared under pressure, and the player chooses clear-order for a reason), or the mound nearest him vents at double rate (same choice, zero new render work). Either gives the parking a job the fight can examine. If neither is wanted, the parking should at least be read as a deliberate tell (he sits by the warren's heart), which costs a line in the Field Guide, not code.

9. Phase 3 leap, the vanish: windup 0.9s crouch showing the '!' windup marker, then 1.2s airborne with hidden set, health held, and nothing drawn at all ("gone up, not gone under: nothing to draw", js/director.js:1323-1339, 1329; js/render.js:761-768). Goal: a beat of pure withheld state, the rabbit is simply not there, which is the eeriest single second the fight has. Grounding: threat emergence from withheld information as a tension device (research/game-systems/20-minutes-till-dawn-experience-ledger/report.md S5 is the library's nearest fact; the leap-specific reading is house reasoning). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON as a beat, but the render is a standing doctrine violation with a mandated fix: MOTION_DESIGN's boss rows order that BRAMBLEBUCK never draw nothing during the leap, with the shadow encoding height, because the body channel of a boss signature is never removable. The eerie absence survives that fix fine: a shadow with no body over it is more wrong, not less.

10. Phase 3 tracking shadow: zone r120, warn 1.3s, dmg 1.2x, knock 220, spawned on the player and closing 75 percent of the remaining gap per second; he lands on it at 1.2s and force-detonates it (js/director.js:1333-1336, 1345-1359). Goal: an attack the player answers with movement timing rather than position, since the comment states the design intent outright: "standing still is the only way to be under it when it lands" (js/director.js:1345-1346), so the correct play is to keep moving and the late dart is a bonus read. Grounding: the ledgers hold no telegraph numbers (absence, flagged); the shadow publishes state continuously, which is lesson-3-compliant (single-room floor S1A). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON, with one honesty defect: warn is 1.3 but detonation comes at 1.2, so the zone's rendered sweep hand never completes (js/director.js:1334 vs 1353-1359). The game's telegraph clock lies by a tenth of a second on the boss that teaches telegraph reading. One number fixes it.

11. Phase 3 landing ring: an expanding ring at speed 300, dmg 0.6x, maxR 380, plus shake 9 and hitstop 0.1 (js/director.js:1361-1363). Goal: a second, differently-shaped consequence layered on the landing so escaping the shadow is not the whole answer, and, better, it is literally the player's own bugle ring aimed the other way ("Same shape as the bugle ring the player owns, aimed the other way", js/director.js:714-715). Grounding: vary which axis drives each threat so consecutive beats feel different (research/audio/music-tension-pacing/report.md S7.2, applied to mechanics by analogy, flagged as analogy). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON. The mirrored-bugle detail deserves to be canon in the Field Guide someday; it is the Signed using scout language back.

12. Phase 3 self-stun, 1.5s after every landing (js/director.js:1367, 1372-1376). Goal: the authored punish window, the fight's rhythm resolved into a guaranteed answer, so the exam ends on "did you survive the pattern long enough to spend the window". Grounding: a hard, legible end to each threat cycle is what makes the cycle payable (research/game-systems/single-room-tension-systems-floor/report.md S1A lesson 5, applied within the fight; application is mine). House doctrine wants recovery in a visibly open pose, the punish window as a picture (MOTION_DESIGN anticipation floors, carried); today the stun renders as an alpha drop to 0.85 and nothing else (js/render.js:815). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON; the picture is missing, and the fix fits the eight-number pose channel without a rig.

13. Sizing: hp 1050 scaled by bossHpScale (1 + m*0.105), damage at half the trash threat rate times the night multiplier (dmg = def.dmg * DATA.dmgScale(m * 0.5) * nightDmgMul(), js/director.js:325; js/data.js:256, 328-329; js/director.js:317-325). At the act 1 boss slot m is 4.175 (threatOf(1,7,8) + 0.5, js/data.js:344-345; js/run.js:944-946; room count 8 per BUILD_ARCHITECTURE, carried), giving about 1510 hp and, on night 1 where nightDmgMul() is 1, about 16 contact, derived from the formulas, not measured. Goal: a first exam priced for a first-night build, where contact hurts enough to teach and never enough to end the lesson in one touch. Grounding: early fights survivable with the starting build, the exam bites later (research/game-systems/slay-the-spire-experience-ledger/report.md S5, by analogy). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON.

14. Boss integrity package: knockback immune, bugle stun and knock skip him, Reveille skips him, burns run 0.6x, slows still work at 0.62 (js/game.js:826, 909, 1893-1902, 2273-2275; js/director.js:1964; all carried from the inventory, not re-read this session). Goal: no build gets to skip the exam by disabling the examiner, while slows keep control builds relevant. Grounding: none in the library (gap); the goal statement is house reasoning. Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON, thinly; if any of these ever needs defending in a balance argument, the defense is the exam framing above and should be written into the doctrine rather than re-derived.

15. Music: boss.bramblebuck, 128bpm G minor, stems stack base / p2 / p3, base must carry the fight alone, and the stems' own stated contract is that the layers join at the phases: base always audible, p2 "phase 2 joins", p3 "phase 3 joins" (js/music/src/boss_bramblebuck.js:21-25); the audio director joins p2 at raw hp 0.66 and p3 at 0.33 for every boss (js/audio_director.js:181-192). Goal of the stems: escalation stepped by fight state so any attempt length lands (research/audio/music-tension-pacing/report.md S8.3 and S10). Verdict on the stems: KEEPS ITS REASON. Verdict on the trigger numbers: hp-fraction triggers are library-sanctioned in general (S8.3 names boss health-percentage thresholds among the most robust escalation triggers, and the S8.5 worked example triggers its plateaus at exactly 66 and 33 percent boss hp). What HAS NO REASON YET is using thresholds that disagree with this boss's actual cuts: his are 0.60/0.25 (js/director.js:343), so p2 music arrives while he is still in phase 1 and p3 while he is still in phase 2, the roar and the music escalate at different moments, and the phase roar gets no stinger at all, which inverts the one phase fact the library holds (transitions marked with stingers, research/game-systems/hades-experience-ledger/report.md S10). The mismatch is between the stems' stated contract (js/music/src/boss_bramblebuck.js:21-25, layers join at phase 2 and phase 3) and the audio director's raw hp triggers (js/audio_director.js:181-192). The reason the trigger COULD carry: drive setPhase from the onBossPhase call site every transition already funnels through (js/director.js:1078), so music, mechanics, and item procs share one clock; S8.3 also lists phase changes as first-class triggers. Also inherited honestly: nothing is audible until Music.load gets a call site (AUDIO_2.1.md, carried), so none of this is player-facing yet.

16. Kill and payout: chest (an instant levelPool pick), cocoa, 40 bonus gems plus xp 60, 'BRAMBLEBUCK IS DOWN', act1boss flag, codex entry, doors open on clear paying 8+act*4 coins (js/game.js:955-996, 693-708; js/room.js:748-811; all carried). Goal: advancement itself plus a no-choice power beat, matching the documented boss reward grammar (research/game-systems/binding-of-isaac-experience-ledger/report.md S5 and S9; research/game-systems/hyper-light-drifter-experience-ledger/report.md S5). Verdict: KEEPS ITS REASON. One dead limb rides along: DATA.COINS.boss (60) has no reader anywhere (js/data.js:554-555, carried; repo grep per inventory), so a boss kill pays no direct coins; wire it or delete it, section 5.

17. Failure: death destroys the continue save, badges bank anyway with the totem multiplier, lastKiller drives Brock's tip "count the hops" at camp (js/game.js:1030, 1058-1064; js/meta.js:107-140; js/dialogue.js:116; all carried). ## 3. WHAT IS MISSING

Goals the library says a boss in this position must serve that no current mechanic serves.

1. The pre-exam trough. The node before the act boss is a rest beat in the canon run game (research/game-systems/slay-the-spire-experience-ledger/report.md S5, documented map rule) and relief is a resource to spend before the peak (research/audio/music-tension-pacing/report.md S7.2); SPD even signals the boss by a music shift as advance warning (research/game-systems/shattered-pixel-dungeon-experience-ledger/report.md S10). BRAMBLEBUCK's room chain has no trough: the den is just the act's last slot, the act combat theme runs to the door, and in room runs even his voice line decodes cold because the 25s pre-warm is field-only (js/director.js:606-614 carried; js/game.js:1329-1330 carried). Nothing before the first exam ever gets quiet.

2. The held breath. Pull the music out right before the strike; silence before the boss's first move lands harder than any crescendo (research/audio/music-tension-pacing/report.md S6.2). His entrance is the loudest moment available (roar, shake 8, hitstop 0.4, flash 0.9, js/director.js:391-395) with no silence on either side of it, and his phase transitions are roar-only with the music change arriving at an unrelated hp fraction (entry 15). The library's one phase fact (stingers mark transitions, hades ledger S10) is unserved.

3. A guaranteed last build decision before the exam. The canon pattern gives the player one final choice at the threshold (rest-or-upgrade, research/game-systems/slay-the-spire-experience-ledger/report.md S5 and S9). MBM banks ranks during the fight and drafts at room clear (BUILD_ARCHITECTURE.md 4.1, carried), which handles the exit well, but nothing guarantees a decision on the way in. This is a run-structure gap that BRAMBLEBUCK merely exposes first; it belongs to LEVEL_DESIGN/BUILD reconciliation, not to his phase machine, and it is listed here so this chapter does not silently absorb it.

4. The stand-here verb is never taught in act 1. MAWMOLE's tunnel plugs and OLD NOBODY's sign contests both examine standing on a spot under pressure (js/director.js:1449-1477, 1751-1799, carried), and the act 1 boss is supposed to teach the exam format the night will use (research/game-design/journey-act-chapter-structure-recipes/report.md S5.2). BRAMBLEBUCK teaches shoot-the-prop, read-the-ground, and wait-for-the-window, but the fourth verb of the boss language debuts in act 2 untaught. Whether to fix this here or accept it is a genuine design call, section 5.

5. The punish window as a picture. House doctrine requires recovery in a visibly open pose; the library cannot help (the ledgers hold no telegraph or recovery numbers, and this chapter did not consult the boss-encounter-recipes tables), but the doctrine is binding and today the window reads only as a rabbit standing still at alpha 0.85 (entry 12, js/render.js:815).

6. Honest statement of the limit: the library holds no doctrine on why phases exist, no adds doctrine, and no telegraph numbers in the ledgers (boss-encounter-recipes S4.5 carries tier timing tables this chapter did not consult). Sections of this chapter that reason about those (entries 3, 6, 7, 10, 12) are house reasoning checked against house doctrine, and any future skeptic pass should treat them as claims of this document, not of the research library.

## 4. THE 3.0 SHAPE

Sharpen (all inherit existing doctrine; none changes what the fight is).

- S1. The leap draws a shadow that encodes height, body never fully absent from the read. This is MOTION_DESIGN's standing mandate, restated here because the fight's best beat currently violates the fight's own teaching job. Cost: about 1 day (guess), render only.
- S2. Make the shadow clock honest: warn 1.2 to match the 1.2s air time, so the sweep hand completes exactly as he lands (js/director.js:1334). Cost: trivial, minutes, plus one playtest look.
- S3. Raise the hopscotch stamp warn from 0.35 to 0.7. This legalizes the world channel under the telegraph grammar and costs almost nothing in threat because the stamp lands where he was, not where you are; if the fight goes soft, stamp every second landing in the back half of phase 1 instead of every third. Cost: 0.2 day (guess) including retune.
- S4. Give the stun a picture: ears flat, body sprawled, via the squash/rot pose channel already rendering him (js/render.js:787-799). No rig, per doctrine. Cost: 0.5 to 1 day (guess).
- S5. Give the phase 2 body a job (entry 8): either in-place landings stamp small local zones at his corner, or his nearest mound vents at double rate. Pick one, not both. Cost: 0.5 to 1 day (guess) including balance pass at the act 1 slot's m=4.175.
- S6. Drive music setPhase from the onBossPhase call site instead of raw hp fractions (entry 15). Shared fix benefiting all five bosses; his chapter claims it because his cut mismatch is the widest teaching harm (music promises an escalation the fight has not made, on the boss teaching players what escalation sounds like). Cost: 0.5 day (guess), audio_director only, degrade path unchanged.
- S7. Replace the 1.2s phase-guard 8Hz strobe with the doctrine-mandated steady overlay or 2.5Hz ramp (js/render.js:823-828). Standalone safety fix, ships regardless of everything else. Cost: 0.5 day (guess).

Cut: nothing. Every mechanic on the ledger either keeps its reason or can be given one at S5's price. Cutting the warren or the leap would demolish the game's grammar school, and the library's named failure modes for this class are import errors, not overbuilt act 1 bosses.

Add (each gated on a section 5 call; none is assumed).

- A1. Teach the stand-here verb: the last living mound cannot be broken, only sealed by standing on it 1.2s (reusing the tunnel-plug interaction pattern). This previews MAWMOLE and OLD NOBODY's contest grammar inside the gentlest fight. Risk: it complicates the cleanest phase in the roster and adds a rule the '!' and zone language cannot carry alone, so it must be readable with no text (attack standard, MOTION_DESIGN section 4). ## 5. OPEN CALLS FOR THE CREATOR

**Call 1: does dying to him keep the book?** Today, implicitly yes: player death forfeits everything he skimmed, no refund path exists (js/director.js:927-938 fires only on his death). **Call 2: who gets the silence?** One boss phase change in the whole game is budgeted as total stillness. This chapter argues for 'THE BOOK IS OPEN'.
